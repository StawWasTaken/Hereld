What it is

A herald announces. That is the whole job.

A herald carried news from one place to another and did not editorialise on the way. Hereld does the same for the work you do: you say it once, and the people following you get it in the order you said it.

Short on purpose

Six hundred characters. Long enough to say what happened and why it mattered, short enough that you will actually write it.

In order, not ranked

Your feed is the people you follow, newest first. Nothing is buried for being unpopular and nothing is pushed for being loud.

Read without joining

Posts and profiles are public. A professional network people cannot read before they sign up is a wall, not a network.

How it works

Three things, and then you are in.

Claim a handle

Three to twenty characters. It is yours, it goes on everything you post, and nobody else can take it.

Say what you do

One line under your name. It travels with every post, so people reading you for the first time know who they are reading.

Follow, endorse, relay

Follow to hear someone. Endorse to say it was worth reading. Relay to carry it to the people following you.

Where it is at

Hereld is new, and small.

It went up this year and it does four things: posts, replies, relays and follows. There are no messages yet, no jobs board, no company pages and no notifications. Those are on the bench, not on the site. If you join now you are early, and early means quiet.

We would rather tell you that than let you find out.
